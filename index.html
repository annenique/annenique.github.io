About

I am especially interested in how science, psychology, and interactive design can come together — whether that is in a game, a research project, or something in between. My work spans immunology research in a university lab and building browser-based biology games from scratch.

My goals are to continue developing my skills in programming and game design, further my education in biology and technology, and find ways to connect the two in work that is genuinely useful to people.
